A Letter For You

My Love,

Happy first monthsary. ❤️

I know one month isn't a very long time. But I don't measure what I feel for you by the number of days we've been together.

What matters to me is how you slowly became someone I look forward to talking to, someone I think about during random moments, and someone whose happiness genuinely matters to me.

The funny thing is, we haven't even met in person yet. And maybe that's what makes our story different. We don't have a hundred pictures together. We don't have memories of walking together, eating together, or watching sunsets side by side.

But we have conversations. We have feelings. We have the little moments that only the two of us understand. And we have the possibility of creating all those memories in the future.

I don't want to rush our story. I just want to enjoy every chapter with you. I want to know you more. I want to understand you. I want to support you. And when the day finally comes that I get to see you and hold you for the first time, I hope I'll be able to tell you, "See? You were worth waiting for."

Thank you for choosing me. Thank you for letting me be part of your life. And thank you for giving me something beautiful to look forward to.

Happy first monthsary, love.

This is only our first chapter.
I hope there are many more pages waiting for us.

Yours, always. ❤️
